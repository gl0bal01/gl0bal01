## Challenge Guidelines

- **Respect the Community**: We're here to learn and have fun together.
- **No Spoilers**: Don't share solutions directly. Help others learn by providing hints.
- **Ask for Hints**: If you're stuck, don't hesitate to ask for guidance in our Discord.
- **Report Issues**: If you encounter any technical problems with a challenge, please let us know.
